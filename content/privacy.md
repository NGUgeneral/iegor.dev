# Privacy & Analytics

This site uses [`doorbell`](https://github.com/NGUgeneral/doorbell), a self-hosted, open-source edge function, to collect minimal, aggregate pageview metrics (page path, device type, referrer, and country).

Incoming IP addresses are processed temporarily in memory strictly to resolve geographic country origin and are never logged, stored, or cross-referenced. No cookies, local storage, or persistent tracking identifiers are used. Processing is based on Legitimate Interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for basic site operational analytics.
